Service 06

Market analysis

Market analysis earns its cost when it changes a decision about a product, a price, a new market or a partner.

Service 06 of 07Led by Shafiki Sabiiti

We look at markets from the side of the investor and from the side of the SACCOs, cooperatives, farmers and households that finance and services are meant to reach.

Shafiki Sabiiti leads this work, with Sebyala Samuel on how finance is priced and structured.

Service lead

Shafiki Sabiiti

Statistician and Data Analyst. BSc Statistics, Makerere University; market segmentation and trade-policy research for USAID and AESA East Africa.

Full profile

Market and feasibility assessments

Demand and supply, competition, pricing and the likely take-up of a product or project, usually with a financial model and business plan.

Sector and value-chain studies

Agriculture, commodity trade, agro-processing and microfinance, including the economics of inputs and commodity prices.

Market segmentation

Grouping customers by location, income and need, so that products and prices can be matched to each group.

Customer and product research

Surveys and interviews used to design savings, loan and insurance products for SACCOs and other financial institutions.

How finance is priced and structured

How lenders, guarantors and development finance institutions set terms. Sebyala Samuel has structured credit guarantee facilities with IFC, GuarantCo, ABSA, Old Mutual and Mastercard.

Policy and trade studies

Reviews of the rules and policies that shape a market, such as agricultural trade regulations across a region.
