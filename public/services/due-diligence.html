Service 01

Due diligence, investment appraisal and valuation

Before committing money, an investor or lender needs to know that the business is what its documents say it is, that the investment makes sense, and what it is worth. This service covers all three.

Service 01 of 07Led by Sebyala Samuel

IntrepidX has carried out more than 20 due diligence assignments for Kampani, an institutional investor based in Belgium, covering financial, governance, legal, technical and commercial due diligence, valuation and investment appraisal. One of them is described under ongoing assignments.

Sebyala Samuel leads this work. He is an investment manager with more than 12 years' experience and over US$150 million of private-sector debt and equity transactions in 15 African and 2 European markets.

Due diligence

Five kinds of due diligence

Due diligenceWhat we look atWhat the client gets
FinancialHistorical financial statements, rebuilt from the audited accounts; quality of earnings; working capital, net debt and debt-like items; tax position and URA compliance; budgets and forecastsReport with adjusted earnings, issues ranked by severity, conditions precedent and outstanding information
CommercialThe market, customers and competitors; pricing and margins; supply chains; whether the sales and growth assumptions in the business plan are realisticAssessment of the plan's market assumptions, with a full market study where needed
GovernanceOwnership and related parties; board make-up and oversight; management capacity; policies, credit processes, internal controls and reportingGovernance assessment with a prioritised action plan
LegalIncorporation and ownership records; licences and permits; material contracts; security over assets; litigation and regulatory complianceLegal due diligence report, prepared with an associate advocate
TechnicalPlant, equipment and processes; capacity and condition; capital expenditure plans; environmental and social complianceTechnical report, prepared with a sector specialist

Legal and technical due diligence are carried out with qualified advocates and technical specialists engaged for each assignment, working under our Team Leader.

Investment appraisal

We prepare and review feasibility studies, financial models and business plans, and test them: how much debt a project can carry, and what happens to returns if prices, volumes or the exchange rate move.

Valuation

Business valuations based on cash-flow models, collateral assessment for credit decisions, and reviews of valuations prepared by others, reconciled to the asset register and accounts. Formal asset valuations are done with an associate registered valuer.
