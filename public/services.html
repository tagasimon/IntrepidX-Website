What we do

Our services

We have six lines of business. Each one stands on its own, with a named lead, and clients can use one or several.

01

Due diligence, investment appraisal and valuation

Financial, commercial, governance, legal and technical due diligence; feasibility studies and financial models; business valuations, collateral assessment and valuation reviews.

Sebyala SamuelService lead 02

Post-investment support

Monitoring of borrowers and investees for lenders and investors, portfolio reviews, hands-on support to the businesses and financial institutions that receive finance, and turnaround plans.

Dickson TuryahabweService lead 03

Monitoring and evaluation

Baseline studies, M&E frameworks and plans, mid-term and end-line evaluations, surveys, organisational capacity assessments, and M&E training.

Prof. Atukunda GershomService lead 04

Impact assessment

Impact evaluations, jobs and income measurement, tracer studies, and impact and ESG measurement for investors and funds.

Dr. Yusuf KiwalaService lead 05

Management information systems

Working out what information managers need, then designing the system, building or integrating the software, setting up dashboards and data collection, and training the people who will run it.

Kazooba SimonService lead 06

Market analysis

Market and feasibility assessments, sector and value-chain studies, market segmentation, customer and product research, and policy and trade studies.

How we staff assignments

A named Team Leader on every assignment

Each assignment has a named Team Leader, who deals with the client and signs off every deliverable after an internal review. The rest of the team is chosen to fit the terms of reference. Legal and technical due diligence and formal valuations use associate specialists.
